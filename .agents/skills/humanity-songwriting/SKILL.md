# 人類観測所の作詞

Read `docs/humanity/MISSION.md` and `docs/humanity/songwriting.md` before changing Humanity lyrics or its generation prompt. Keep the selected genre, tempo, key, arrangement, and vocal direction intact when the work is a lyric-only revision.

Design the lyric world together with the song's central feeling and sound. Let each song choose its subject, characters, relationship, point of view, setting, length, structure, musical direction, and ending. The user likes the musical direction and sound design on [IMOJIST's channel](https://www.youtube.com/@IMOJIST); treat it as a preference signal, not a genre or arrangement rule. A spoken phrase, a word's sound, a human relationship, an unusual image, or a supplied brief may be the starting point. Use wordplay, surprise, metaphor, repetition, rhyme, or contrast between sound and feeling when they help this song; none is required.

Treat published interviews and songs as evidence for broad creative principles, not templates. Do not reuse lyrics, distinctive phrases, characters, plot, or imagery. Keep the resulting work's language and world original to Humanity.

Listen for musical fit, singability, a distinct world and listener experience, emotional movement, and a memorable aftertaste. Use these observations to find worthwhile revisions, not as a fixed checklist, numerical gate, or automatic rejection. Humanity's warmth toward people can inform any subject without forcing every song to be about awkwardness. If there is a melody, compare alternatives against that same melody and tempo; report the comparison as unverified until the versions have actually been heard.

The current YuE2 pipeline creates lyrics and music together. Do not claim that it preserves an existing melody during lyric revision. When using that pipeline, write an original new song and leave the comparison to existing melodies open until a fixed-melody input is available.
