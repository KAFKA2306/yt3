# 人類観測所の作詞

Read `docs/humanity/MISSION.md` and `docs/humanity/songwriting.md` before changing Humanity lyrics or its generation prompt. Keep the selected genre, tempo, key, arrangement, and vocal direction intact when the work is a lyric-only revision.

Start from `docs/humanity/story-world.json`, the shared world and relationship canon for the label. Give every song a clear concept and an event listeners can follow on first hearing. Let characters and relationships develop across songs while keeping each song understandable on its own. HoneyWorks is a preference signal for accessible character relationships and storytelling, not a template. The user also likes the worldbuilding and lyric design associated with DECO*27 and the musical direction and sound design on [IMOJIST's channel](https://www.youtube.com/@IMOJIST); treat these as preference signals. Do not reuse lyrics, distinctive phrases, characters, plots, melodies, or signature presentation.

Let each song choose its subject, point of view, setting within the shared world, length, structure, musical direction, and ending. Romance, wordplay, metaphor, rhyme, and contrast between sound and feeling are optional. Keep the resulting work original to Humanity.

Use JIZURA as a story-aware kinetic lyric visualizer: choose one of its supported themes and an accent palette from the song's emotional movement, let JIZURA analyze the finished audio for beat sync, and use repeated-line unification or readable typesetting when they suit the song. Preserve the rendered JIZURA project with the video. Do not describe it as character animation.

Listen for musical fit, singability, clear relationships, emotional movement, and a memorable aftertaste. Use these observations to find worthwhile revisions, not as a fixed checklist, numerical gate, or automatic rejection. If there is a melody, compare alternatives against that same melody and tempo; report the comparison as unverified until the versions have actually been heard.

The current YuE2 pipeline creates lyrics and music together. Do not claim that it preserves an existing melody during lyric revision. When using that pipeline, write an original new song and leave the comparison to existing melodies open until a fixed-melody input is available.
