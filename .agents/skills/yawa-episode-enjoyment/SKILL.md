# Yawa recurring episode experience

Read `docs/yawa/MISSION.md`, `docs/yawa/characters.json`, and `docs/yawa/voice-preferences.md` as authoritative before reviewing or revising a Yawa Archive episode.

Yawa Archive is an **all-ages, advertiser-friendly (YouTube green-icon) situation-voice theater** built around a fixed 3-character cast (`haruka`, `shizuku`, `nao`) with persistent per-character reference voices and cohesive anime-style character thumbnails. Optimize for why a listener clicks, stays through an 8–15 minute episode, and subscribes to hear that character again.

The installed `sakurai` skill is a design reference, not a comedy rubric. When its principles help (early hook, pacing, payoff, variety), translate them to audio drama without literal game mechanics.

## Review an episode

1. **Cast authenticity**: Check that the dialogue, distance, and `acting` fit the chosen character in `docs/yawa/characters.json` and feel distinct from the other two cast members.
2. **Anti-repetition & genre balance**: Compare against recent episodes. Flag recurrence of the same cast member or fallback into "missed last train / midnight care / bedtime whispering" tropes. Encourage healthy rotation across light comedy, banter, outings/mysteries, and warm reassurance.
3. **Listener promise & pacing**: State the episode's promise in one sentence. Check whether the opening establishes the character and situation promptly, delivers concrete sensory/emotional beats, and lands a memorable afterglow.
4. **Delivery & advertiser-friendliness**: Keep delivery warm, clear, and conversational with natural pauses — no exaggerated breath cues, ear-blowing, or sexually suggestive roleplay/metadata that risks YouTube limited ads (yellow icon).
5. **Packaging coherence**: Check that the title, description, tags, and character `thumbnail_prompt` promise the exact experience delivered in the script without clickbait or policy-risk keywords.

When asked to improve a draft, name the single highest-impact change first, then revise only what is needed. Do not add automated retries or publication gates unless explicitly requested.
